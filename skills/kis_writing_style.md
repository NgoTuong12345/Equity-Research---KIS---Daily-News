# KIS Writing Style Guide and EN-VN Conventional Rules

Use this guide when writing, editing, or translating financial research content for KIS Vietnam Securities. It combines the core writing style guide with English-Vietnamese conventional translation rules from the attached documents.

---

## Voice & Tone

- **Active voice always.** Example: "HPG will expand capacity" — never "Capacity will be expanded by HPG."
- **Possessives over prepositional phrases.** Example: "VCB's NIM expanded" — not "the NIM of VCB expanded."
- **No contractions.** Write "do not", "will not", "it is" — never "don't", "won't", "it's."
- **Avoid weak adverbs ending in "-ly"** when a stronger verb exists. Replace "significantly increased" with "surged."
- **Use a confident, forward-looking, institutional tone.** Write like a sell-side analyst: concise, data-driven, and direct.

---

## Sentence & Paragraph Structure

- **Bold the opening sentence** of each section or subsection when it contains the lead forecast or key takeaway.
- **Use inline numbered clauses** for multi-driver explanations, for example: "…driven by 1) higher NIM, 2) controlled credit costs and 3) strong fee income."
- Keep paragraphs tight: 3–5 sentences max in analytical sections.
- Avoid excessive white space.

## Numbers, Units & Formatting

| Convention | Correct | Incorrect |
|---|---|---|
| Forecast suffix | 2026F | 2026E |
| Valuation format | 10x 2026F PE | 10x 2026E P/E |
| Approximate | ~12%, ~VND1,100tn | approx. 12% |
| Ranges | KRW10bn–15bn, 2024–2026 | KRW10bn~15bn |
| Billions/trillions | bn, tn | bln, trn, mln |
| Millions | mn | mln |
| Spell out 1–9 in prose | three-year CAGR, six quarters | 3-year CAGR, 6 quarters |
| Percentage | 15.1% YoY, 8.5% QoQ | 15.1 % YoY |
| Fractions | 1.5x | 1½x |
| Chart axis labels | (L) / (R) | (LHS) / (RHS) |
| Consensus | the consensus | consensus |

## Titles & Headings

- **Sentence case only.** Capitalize only the first word, for example: "Ambitions in the national rise era".
- Keep titles at six words or fewer when possible.
- Section titles use brand blue `#5692CE` in formatted reports.

---

## Tables & Figures

- Number sequentially: `Table 1.`, `Table 2.`, `Figure 1.`, `Figure 2.`
- Table and figure titles go above and outside, left-aligned.
- Unit labels such as `(VND bn, %)` go above and outside, right-aligned.
- Source lines go below and outside: `Source: Korea Investment & Securities`.
- Note lines use `Note:` and are never italic.
- Table headers use bold text on light blue `#E1E8F7` background.
- Table body numbers are right-aligned; body text is left-aligned.
- Use minimal borders: horizontal dividers only, open sides.

## Ratings Definitions

### Stock ratings

- **BUY** — Expected total return ≥15%
- **Hold** — Expected total return between –5% and +15%
- **Sell** — Expected total return ≤–5%

### Sector ratings

- **Overweight** — Increase sector weighting vs. VNIndex market-cap weight
- **Neutral** — Maintain sector weighting in line with VNIndex
- **Underweight** — Reduce sector weighting vs. VNIndex market-cap weight

*KIS does not offer target prices for stocks with Hold or Sell ratings.*

## EN to VN Translation Rules

### Institutions and organizations

- `State Bank of Vietnam` → `NHNN (Ngân hàng Nhà nước Việt Nam)`
- `Federal Reserve` → `FED` or `Cục Dự trữ Liên bang Mỹ`
- `General Statistics Office` → `GSO (Tổng cục Thống kê)`
- `Ministry of Finance/Treasury` → `KBNN (Kho bạc Nhà nước)`
- `Hanoi Stock Exchange` → `HNX`
- `Vietnam Bond Market Association` → `VBMA`

### Financial and monetary terms

- `repo` → `hợp đồng mua kỳ hạn`
- `interbank rate` → `lãi suất liên ngân hàng`
- `G-bond (Government bond)` → `TPCP (Trái phiếu Chính phủ)`
- `yield` → `lợi suất`
- `liquidity withdrawal` → `rút ròng thanh khoản`
- `injection` → `bơm thanh khoản`
- `basis points (bps)` → `điểm cơ bản (đcb)`
- `percentage points (ppts)` → `điểm phần trăm`
- `volume` → `sản lượng`
- `ASPs` → `giá bán TB`
- `EBT` → `LNTT`
- `NPAT` → `LNST`
- `NPAT-MI` → `LNST-CTM`

### Time and tenor

- `1-week tenor` → `kỳ hạn 1 tuần`
- `overnight (ON)` → `qua đêm`
- `short/medium/long-term` → `ngắn hạn / trung hạn / dài hạn`

## Report Structure

1. Cover page (title, TOC, branding)
2. Economy — Executive summary + macro outlook
3. Economy — Thematic subsections (public investment, consumption, exports)
4. Economy — Macro scorecard table
5. Strategy — Market performance review
6. Strategy — Market upgrade / catalysts
7. Strategy — Valuation & cycle analysis
8. Sector outlook — Summary table
9. Individual sector pages (each with `▶ Three keywords` box + rating)

- [ ] Active voice throughout
- [ ] No contractions

- [ ] Sentence case on all titles

- [ ] Source and note lines are not italicized
- [ ] Tables: titles outside, headers light blue, numbers right-aligned
- [ ] Rating definitions included at end of report
